Privacy Policy

TrueTune — Tuner & Metronome

Last updated: July 10, 2026

TrueTune collects no data. None.

TrueTune collects no personal data, has no accounts, and sends nothing off your device. The tuner and metronome run entirely on your phone. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

Microphone

When you use the tuner, the app listens through your device microphone to detect the pitch of the note you play. This audio is analyzed on your device, in real time, and is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted anywhere. The microphone is released the moment you stop the tuner or leave the tuner screen. The app requests microphone access only when you start the tuner, and explains why at that moment. If you decline, the metronome and the rest of the app still work.

Data we collect

None. No personal information, no usage analytics, no device identifiers, no location, no contacts. The app has no user accounts and no server — there is nothing to sign into and nothing to delete, because nothing about you is ever collected or stored off your device. Your settings (reference pitch, tunings, theme, etc.) are saved only in local app storage on your device.

Network

The core app works fully offline. The app itself does not request internet access. The only time anything leaves the app is if you choose to make a one-time Supporter Pack purchase (handled entirely by the Google Play Store, subject to Google's own privacy policy — we never see your payment details) or, where offered, tap an optional link to an external support page, which opens in your browser (subject to that page's own privacy policy).

Children's privacy

TrueTune is a general-audience music utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
